Larry Joe Bird, nicknamed "The Hick from French Lick", was born on
December 7, 1956, in Indiana. He is regarded as one of the greatest
basketball players of all time and one of the best shooters in the history
of the sport.

## Arrival in Boston

Although the Boston Celtics drafted him sixth overall in the 1978 NBA Draft,
Bird chose to play one more season in college before joining the pros for
the 1979–80 season. In his very first year he won Rookie of the Year.

## The golden era and MVP awards

Over his thirteen-year career, spent entirely in a Boston Celtics jersey,
Bird won three NBA titles (1981, 1984, and 1986) and three consecutive MVP
awards (1984–1986).

## The Dream Team and life after playing

The crowning achievement of his playing career came in 1992 at the Barcelona
Olympics as a member of the original "Dream Team". Larry Bird is the only
person in NBA history to win Rookie of the Year, league MVP, Coach of the
Year, and Executive of the Year.
